Why enterprises need this

Legacy systems frequently encode business rules in implementation details that are easy to disturb during AI-assisted refactoring.

Legacy risk

Behavior hides in implementation

Tax rules, tier thresholds, rounding, refunds, ledger effects, audit events, and state transitions may not be fully documented.

AI risk

Green tests can still miss drift

A candidate can be cleaner and pass ordinary tests while silently changing a boundary condition or financial outcome.

Aegis answer

Separate transformation from acceptance

Bob changes the software. Aegis checks the evidence. A human decides whether to merge.
